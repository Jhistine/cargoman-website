Platform overview

One Platform. Web, Desktop and Mobile.

CargoMan follows the operational movement from pre-advice and arrival through weighing, handling, stock, evidence and dispatch. Web, desktop and mobile applications provide the right interface for each part of the operation while working from the same connected data.

What CargoMan controls

CargoMan Controls What Actually Happens on Site.

Your ERP may know what was ordered. Your accounting system knows what was invoiced. Your transport system may know what was planned.

CargoMan controls what actually happens on site - who arrived, what was weighed, what was received, where cargo was handled, what was packed, what left, and what evidence supports the movement.

CargoMan can integrate with upstream and downstream systems while providing the operational control layer between planning and physical execution.

The operating model

A Connected Workflow, Not a Collection of Screens.

Not every deployment uses every step below - CargoMan is configured around the operation.

1

Plan the Movement

Create orders, booking references, order lines, cargo details, expected trucks, and other pre-advice information.

2

Control Arrival

Identify the vehicle, driver, transporter, client, and expected movement at the gate, with entry and security checks recorded.

3

Capture Weight

Process weighbridge transactions, collect gross/tare/nett values, and associate the reading with the correct record.

4

Execute the Warehouse or Yard Operation

Receive cargo, scan or assign barcodes, allocate lots and orders, record quantities and weights, and pack containers.

5

Capture Proof

Photos, signatures, documents, timestamps, users, and exceptions are recorded alongside the transaction itself.

6

Release and Dispatch

Validate the movement, confirm items, capture final weights or container status, and record exit.

7

Monitor and Report

View live status, stock movement, balances, throughput, KPIs, client-specific information, audit history, and supporting transaction documents.

Product surfaces

Web, Desktop, and Mobile - One Connected Record.

Web Application

Browser-based access for operational management, dashboards, administration, reporting, client visibility, and workflows suited to office or tablet use.

Desktop Application

Supports operational environments where direct hardware integration, peripherals, fast repetitive capture, or resilient site infrastructure is important.

Mobile Application

Puts scanning, validation, photographs, damages, signatures, and transaction capture in the hands of the operator where the cargo actually moves.

Mobile capture

Scanning and Rapid Capture, Built for the Floor.

  • Scan barcodes, containers, vehicles, and documents
  • Perform cargo receiving and dispatch transactions
  • Capture container full-in, packed, full-out, and related lifecycle events
  • Record quantities, weights, lots, and client references
  • Capture damages, exceptions, photographs, and signatures
  • Work with locally available operational data and synchronise when connected

Multi-site operation

Run Each Site Locally. See the Operation Centrally.

CargoMan can support geographically distributed operations while maintaining site-level execution and central visibility. Relevant operational data can synchronise between locations so local teams can continue working without turning every site into an isolated system.

This allows a group to standardise operational records and reporting while still configuring workflows around the realities of individual sites.

Integration layer

CargoMan Fits Around the Systems You Already Use.

CargoMan does not need to replace your ERP, accounting platform, customer system, or existing planning tools. Operational instructions and master data can be brought into CargoMan, while completed movements and reporting data can be exposed or sent to other systems where required.

ODATA-STYLE QUERYING

Authenticated querying for filtering, sorting, selecting, and retrieving operational records.

PURPOSE-BUILT ENDPOINTS

REST endpoints for business processes that don't fit a generic data query.

INTEGRATION USERS

Role-based integration users with token-secured access.

SQL VIEWS

Database-level reporting or exchange where that is the best fit.

CargoMan can also post configured operational data to a customer-owned endpoint, with site- or project-specific integration where needed.

Control points

CargoMan Doesn't Just Record the Movement. It Controls the Handovers.

Each stage can validate what should happen next before the movement progresses. Rules can be configured around the requirements of the site while keeping the operational record structured and auditable.

Expected truck → Gate validation → Weighbridge transaction → Receiving confirmation → Packing / stock validation → Dispatch validation

Configuration, not rebuilds

One Platform, Configured for Different Operations.

CargoMan uses a common platform with configurable workflows, permissions, fields, validation, reports, and integrations. Individual sites can operate differently without turning every deployment into a completely separate system.

Hardware integration

Connect the Software to the Physical Operation.

CargoMan can integrate with weighbridge equipment, scanners, printers, and site-specific operational hardware where required, allowing information to be captured as part of the live workflow instead of re-entered afterwards.
