Platform overview

One Operational Record - Three Ways to Reach It.

CargoMan follows cargo from an expected arrival through to dispatch and reporting. The same record is available through the web, desktop, and mobile surfaces, so every team works from the same operational truth.

The operating model

A Connected Workflow, Not a Collection of Screens.

Not every deployment uses every step below - CargoMan is configured around the operation.

1

Plan the Movement

Create orders, booking references, order lines, cargo details, expected trucks, and other pre-advice information.

2

Control Arrival

Identify the vehicle, driver, transporter, client, and expected movement at the gate, with entry and security checks recorded.

3

Capture Weight

Process weighbridge transactions, collect gross/tare/nett values, and associate the reading with the correct record.

4

Execute the Warehouse or Yard Operation

Receive cargo, scan or assign barcodes, allocate lots and orders, record quantities and weights, and pack containers.

5

Capture Proof

Photos, signatures, documents, timestamps, users, and exceptions are recorded alongside the transaction itself.

6

Release and Dispatch

Validate the movement, confirm items, capture final weights or container status, and record exit.

7

Monitor and Report

View live status, stock movement, balances, throughput, KPIs, client-specific information, audit history, and supporting transaction documents.

Product surfaces

Web, Desktop, and Mobile - One Connected Record.

Web Application

Browser-based access to operational data, management views, client portals, reporting, administration, and workflows suited to desktop or tablet use.

Desktop Application

Supports operationally intensive, site-connected use cases, including environments where direct hardware or local infrastructure integration matters.

Mobile Application

Built for warehouse, yard, gate, and field operators - scan-first capture rather than office-style data entry, with offline-capable workflows.

Mobile capture

Scanning and Rapid Capture, Built for the Floor.

  • Scan barcodes, containers, vehicles, and documents
  • Perform cargo receiving and dispatch transactions
  • Capture container full-in, packed, full-out, and related lifecycle events
  • Record quantities, weights, lots, and client references
  • Capture damages, exceptions, photographs, and signatures
  • Work with locally available operational data and synchronise when connected

Integration layer

Controlled Data Access, Described Plainly.

CargoMan provides controlled data access and integration options without publishing security-sensitive implementation detail.

ODATA-STYLE QUERYING

Authenticated querying for filtering, sorting, selecting, and retrieving operational records.

PURPOSE-BUILT ENDPOINTS

REST endpoints for business processes that don't fit a generic data query.

INTEGRATION USERS

Role-based integration users with token-secured access.

SQL VIEWS

Database-level reporting or exchange where that is the best fit.

CargoMan can also post required data to a customer-owned endpoint, with site- or project-specific integration where needed.
