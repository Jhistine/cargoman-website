Platform overview

One Platform. Web, Desktop and Mobile.

CargoMan follows the operational movement from pre-advice and arrival through weighing, handling, stock, evidence and dispatch. Web, desktop and mobile applications provide the right interface for each part of the operation while working from the same connected data.

What CargoMan controls

CargoMan Controls What Actually Happens on Site.

Your ERP knows what was ordered. CargoMan controls what actually happened - who arrived, what was weighed, what was received, and what left - while integrating with the systems around it.

The operating model

A Connected Workflow, Not a Collection of Screens.

Not every deployment uses every step below - CargoMan is configured around the operation.

1

Plan the Movement

Orders, booking references, cargo details, and expected trucks, captured before arrival.

2

Control Arrival

Identify the vehicle, driver, and transporter at the gate, with entry checks recorded.

3

Capture Weight

Process weighbridge transactions and link the reading to the correct record.

4

Execute the Warehouse or Yard Operation

Receive cargo, scan barcodes, allocate lots, and record quantities and weights.

5

Capture Proof

Photos, signatures, and documents recorded alongside the transaction itself.

6

Release and Dispatch

Validate the movement, confirm items, and record exit.

7

Monitor and Report

Live status, stock movement, throughput, and audit history, with supporting documents.

Product surfaces

Web, Desktop, and Mobile - One Connected Record.

Web Application

Browser-based access for operational management, dashboards, administration, reporting, client visibility, and workflows suited to office or tablet use.

Desktop Application

Supports operational environments where direct hardware integration, peripherals, fast repetitive capture, or resilient site infrastructure is important.

Mobile Application

Puts scanning, validation, photographs, damages, signatures, and transaction capture in the hands of the operator where the cargo actually moves.

Mobile capture

Scanning and Rapid Capture, Built for the Floor.

  • Scan barcodes, containers, vehicles, and documents
  • Perform cargo receiving and dispatch transactions
  • Capture container full-in, packed, full-out, and related lifecycle events
  • Record quantities, weights, lots, and client references
  • Capture damages, exceptions, photographs, and signatures
  • Work with locally available operational data and synchronise when connected

Integration layer

CargoMan Fits Around the Systems You Already Use.

CargoMan does not need to replace your ERP or accounting platform. Operational instructions can be brought in, while completed movements and reporting data can be exposed to other systems.

ODATA-STYLE QUERYING

Authenticated querying for filtering, sorting, and retrieving records.

PURPOSE-BUILT ENDPOINTS

REST endpoints for processes that don't fit a generic query.

INTEGRATION USERS

Role-based integration users with token-secured access.

SQL VIEWS

Database-level reporting or exchange where that's the best fit.
