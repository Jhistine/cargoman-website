About

CargoMan Is Built by RapidDev.

RapidDev is a South African software development company. CargoMan is our logistics execution platform, built for cargo terminals, warehouses, and weighbridge operations across the region.

CargoMan connects the gate, weighbridge, warehouse, mobile teams, and reporting into one operational record - built for the way cargo actually moves through a site, not adapted from generic inventory software.

How we work

Operationally Credible, Practical, and Direct.

Operationally Credible

CargoMan is shaped by real gate, weighbridge, and warehouse workflows - not theoretical inventory management.

Confident and Direct

Practical and Grounded

Configuration adapts to the realities of each site, while records stay structured and auditable.
