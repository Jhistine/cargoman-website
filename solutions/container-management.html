The operational problem

Container Condition Disputes Need Evidence, Fast.

When a container's condition or contents are disputed, the operator needs the photo, the weight, and the seal number - not a memory of what happened.

Without Connected Container Tracking

  • Photos live on someone's phone, not attached to the container record
  • Seal numbers are written down separately from the packing detail
  • Weights are captured on a different system to the container event
  • Reconstructing a container's history means chasing several people

With CargoMan

  • Photos attach directly to the container event they document
  • Seal, booking reference, and vessel detail are captured together
  • Gross and nett weight are part of the same packed-container record
  • Full status history is available from one container lookup

How the CargoMan workflow works

From Empty Container to Sealed, Packed, and Dispatched.

  1. Container Full-In

    An incoming container is recorded against the truck, driver, and order, with photos of its condition on arrival.

  2. Packing

    Booking reference, container number, vessel information, and gross/nett weight are captured as items are packed.

  3. Photo Evidence

    A configurable checklist - container photos, doors open, doors closed, side views - records the packed condition.

  4. Seal and Close

    Line seal detail is recorded and the packing record is saved, ready for dispatch.

  5. Container Full-Out

    The outbound movement is recorded, completing the container's status history for that cycle.
