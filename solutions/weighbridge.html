The operational problem

Weighbridge Tickets Shouldn't Be a Side Process.

When the weighbridge runs on its own paper slips or a standalone terminal, weights drift away from the order, client, and cargo record they belong to.

Without a Connected Weighbridge

  • Clerks retype vehicle, client, and order details already captured at the gate
  • Duplicate or missing tickets surface only during month-end reconciliation
  • Gross/tare/nett figures live on paper or in a separate weighbridge terminal
  • Linking a weight back to the order it belongs to is a manual lookup

With CargoMan

  • Vehicle, transporter, client, and order context is looked up, not retyped
  • Duplicate-ticket prevention runs at the point of capture
  • Gross, tare, and nett values attach directly to the transaction record
  • Every reading is searchable against the order, client, or cargo it supported

How the CargoMan workflow works

From Vehicle Identification to a Linked, Reportable Transaction.

  1. Identify the Vehicle and Context

    The transaction is opened against the truck, transporter, client, and - where a pre-advisement or order exists - the expected movement.

  2. Capture the First Weight

    Gross or tare is recorded for the inbound or outbound leg, with lookups reducing manual entry.

  3. Prevent Duplicates

    CargoMan checks for an existing open transaction for the vehicle before a second ticket can be raised.

  4. Capture the Second Weight

    The matching weight completes the transaction and CargoMan calculates the nett figure.

  5. Link to Cargo and Stock

    The completed weight transaction associates with the relevant order, cargo, and - where configured - stock movement.

  6. Print or Distribute the Ticket

    Ticket layouts are configurable, with printing or electronic distribution where set up for the site.
