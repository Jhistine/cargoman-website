Privacy Policy

Draft — requires legal review before publication

This is a placeholder policy for development purposes. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must not be treated as RapidDev's final privacy notice.

What this covers

This policy describes how RapidDev collects and uses information submitted through the CargoMan website (cargoman.co.za), including the contact and demo request form. It does not cover the CargoMan product itself, which is governed by the agreement in place with each deployment's customer.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact or demo request form, we collect the information you provide: name, work email, company, phone number (if given), type of operation, location, and any details you share about your requirement.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a demo or consultation
  • To understand your operation well enough to show you relevant capability
  • To keep a record of enquiries for our own follow-up

We do not sell or rent the information you submit.

Cookies and analytics

If analytics is enabled on this site, it will be implemented in a privacy-conscious way and limited to understanding site usage and conversion paths — not to build individual visitor profiles. Non-essential tracking will only run with consent where legally required.

Data retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to it and maintain a record of the relationship, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@rapiddev.co.za.
