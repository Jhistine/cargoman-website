Industries

Built for real cargo sites.

CargoMan is shaped around the operations of dry-bulk and break-bulk terminals, warehouses, container depots, mining logistics, and multi-site groups that need central visibility with local execution.

Terminals & depots

Coordinate trucks, cargo, weighing, storage, and dispatch through one connected workflow — for dry-bulk, break-bulk, and general cargo terminals and logistics depots.

Warehouses & storage facilities

Maintain item, lot, order, and evidence-based control from receiving through to dispatch, with printable GRNs, GDNs, and stock balance visibility.

Mining & mineral logistics

Track expected movements, origin data, weights, and site handovers with a clear, auditable record across mining and mineral logistics sites.

Container operations

Connect container events — full-in, packing, full-out — to cargo, trucks, seals, photos, and signatures in one configurable history.

CargoMan also supports port-adjacent and cross-border logistics operations, transport and distribution operators with controlled cargo sites, businesses operating weighbridges as part of receiving or dispatch, and multi-site logistics groups requiring central visibility with local execution.
