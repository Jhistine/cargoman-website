Solutions

Six connected modules for the cargo journey.

Each solution below covers one part of the operation — matched movements, weight capture, receiving and dispatch, container events, mobile execution, and reporting. They all write to the same operational record.

01

Gate & access control

Manage the operational record of people and vehicles entering and leaving a site, matched against what was expected.

  • Arrival, entry, and exit timestamps
  • Gate and exit-gate recording
  • Vehicle and driver information
  • Visit or movement type
  • Site, client, order, and transporter context
  • Security questions and checks
  • Linkage to expected-truck records
  • Current on-site status
  • Controlled access based on user role
  • Full audit history of changes

Operational access control is distinct from physical security hardware — barrier, biometric, ANPR, or licence-disc integrations are confirmed per deployment, not assumed.

02

Weighbridge operations

End-to-end weighbridge transaction processing, designed to integrate with a range of weighbridge environments.

  • Gross, tare, and nett weight capture
  • Inbound and outbound weighing workflows
  • Association to trucks, transporters, clients, orders, and cargo
  • Reduced manual entry through lookups and context
  • Duplicate-ticket prevention
  • Configurable ticket and report layouts
  • Ticket printing and electronic distribution where configured
  • Stock and cargo movement linkage
  • VGM-related capture where required
  • Historical transaction search and reporting

Designed to integrate with most common weighbridge environments, subject to interface assessment — not every indicator is guaranteed compatible.

03

Warehouse & stock

Controlled movement of cargo through receiving, storage, packing, and dispatch — from bulk-style quantities to individually traceable items.

  • Product receiving and dispatching
  • Item- and barcode-level control
  • Client barcode and internal barcode support
  • Lot management
  • Quantities, gross weight, nett weight, and related measurements
  • Warehouse and zone allocation
  • Stock status and balance tracking
  • Packing workflows
  • Damage and exception capture
  • Printable GRNs, GDNs, and operational reports
04

Container lifecycle

Configurable container lifecycle tracking, connecting container events to cargo, vehicles, orders, and evidence.

  • Empty-container movements
  • Container full-in, packing, and full-out
  • Container numbers and seal information
  • Truck, driver, transporter, order, and site association
  • Packed-item detail
  • Damage records
  • Photos and signatures
  • Status history and supporting documents
05

Mobile & offline execution

Scan-first workflows for warehouse, yard, gate, and field operators, with offline-capable operation that synchronises when connectivity is available.

  • Operators capture work where the work happens
  • Scan-first workflows reduce manual entry
  • Local operational data supports continued work during interruptions
  • Records synchronise between site and central environments
  • Background evidence upload for photo-heavy workflows
  • Conflict handling protects server truth and flags exceptions
  • Confirmed transactions can be locked to protect record integrity
06

Reporting & integration

Operational data converted into usable management information, with controlled options to connect CargoMan to the systems around it.

  • Live and historical dashboards
  • Receive and dispatch transactions
  • Stock movement and order balances
  • Truck arrivals, site occupancy, and turnaround information
  • Container status and movements
  • Exceptions, damages, and incomplete processes
  • KPI management with client-, site-, order-, and date-based filtering
  • Export to common formats such as Excel, PDF, and CSV where supported

Integration options include authenticated OData-style querying, purpose-built REST endpoints, role-based integration users, and SQL views — see the platform overview for detail.
