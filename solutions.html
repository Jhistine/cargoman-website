Operational solutions

Where Cargo Physically Moves Through Your Site.

1 · GATE

Gate & Access Control

Match trucks to pre-advised movements, complete entry checks, and maintain a live view of vehicles on site.

2 · WEIGHBRIDGE

Weighbridge Operations

Capture the right weight against the right vehicle, order, or container - with controlled sequences and duplicate-ticket prevention.

3 · WAREHOUSE

Warehouse & Stock

Know what you received, what you still hold, and exactly what left - with traceability down to item or barcode level.

4 · CONTAINERS

Container Operations

Connect the container, cargo, seal, weight, and evidence through each stage of the movement, from full-in to full-out.

Platform capabilities

What Connects Every Movement.

1 · MOBILE

Mobile & Offline Execution

Scan-first capture, photos, and signatures in the hands of the operating team. Supported workflows continue offline and synchronise when connected.

2 · REPORTING

Reporting & Analytics

Reporting generated from the same transactions used to run the operation - not a separate reconciliation exercise.

3 · INTEGRATION

Integration

Fits around your ERP, accounting, and planning tools via controlled APIs, query access, and SQL views.

4 · MULTI-SITE

Multi-Site Operation

Run each site locally while management sees the operation centrally, with consolidated reporting across locations.

5 · EVIDENCE

Evidence & Audit

Photos, signatures, and damage notes attach to the transaction itself - not a separate trail to reconstruct later.

6 · CONFIGURATION

Configuration & Control

Configurable workflows, permissions, and validation rules adapt to the site while records stay structured and auditable.
